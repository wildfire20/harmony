Harmony Finance — Final Pre-Rollout Stabilization Phase

We have completed an end-to-end QA audit of the Harmony Learning Institute Parent/Admin finance system.

The synthetic R0.01 QA payment has now been reversed and cleaned up.

This phase must address the confirmed audit findings before Parent finance is considered rollout-ready.

Do not deploy automatically.
Do not run migrations automatically.
Do not silently rewrite historical finance records.
Do not fabricate historical service charges from current enrollment flags.
Do not change real learner balances unless required by an explicitly audited correction workflow.

The authoritative finance ledger remains the source of truth.

1. Create one canonical payable-obligations service

Parent Submit Proof, Admin allocation adjustment and approval currently do not always derive payable obligations in the same way.

Create one shared backend service/function responsible for:

getPayableObligations(studentId)

or equivalent.

This service must power:

Parent payment choices
Admin allocation targets
Server-side approval validation

The same obligation must have the same identity everywhere.

Each returned obligation should include enough authoritative identity such as:

obligation type
category
invoice_id
invoice_line_item_id where applicable
one_off_fee_id where applicable
assignment_id where applicable
billing period
description
due date
gross amount
discount
net due
amount allocated
amount outstanding
legacy/reconciliation state

Do not let three separate frontend/backend paths independently decide what is payable.

2. Restore legitimate historical Tuition obligations

QA confirmed that the learner has genuine recurring outstanding balances but Parent Submit Proof shows no recurring options.

Historical invoices may predate invoice_line_items.

Support old invoice-level Tuition obligations only when persisted historical evidence safely identifies the invoice as Tuition/school fees.

Do not require a modern line item for these legitimate legacy Tuition invoices.

Preserve:

oldest unpaid Tuition first

behaviour.

Example outstanding recurring balances from the QA fixture:

April       R950
July        R200
August    R2,350
September R2,350

These should be accounted for by the payable-obligation layer without changing their historical amounts.

If an old invoice cannot safely be identified as Tuition, mark it:

Requires reconciliation

rather than guessing.

3. Do not infer Boarding / Transport / Aftercare debt from flags

Enrollment flags describe what the learner is enrolled in.

They are not proof of an existing charge.

Therefore:

Boarding checked

does not automatically mean:

Boarding R1,600 outstanding

Current and future recurring billing must create persisted authoritative service charge lines.

Parent and Admin may only allocate to services backed by a real charge.

Preserve the existing Reconcile Charge feature for legitimate missing charges confirmed by Admin.

4. Fix outstanding one-off fee visibility

QA confirmed that an R1,200 outstanding one-off invoice existed while it was absent from Parent payable choices.

Do not hide an outstanding ledger obligation solely because the original one-off fee definition later becomes inactive/archived.

Existing debt remains an obligation even if the template/definition is no longer active.

Parent behaviour:

UNPAID
→ visible and payable

PARTIALLY_PAID
→ visible with remaining balance

PENDING_REVIEW
→ visible as pending, not selectable again

PAID
→ hidden from "Optional Fees to Include"

REQUIRES_RECONCILIATION
→ visible with a clear non-payable explanation where appropriate

Paid one-off fees must remain in:

payment history
audit history
one-off reconciliation
receipt history

10. Fix discount stacking

QA reproduced a case where:

Generated discount lines = R250

The later net-due clamp hides the incorrect breakdown.

Fix calculateApprovedDiscounts() or equivalent.

Every discount must be capped by BOTH:

remaining invoice-level discount capacity

and:

remaining target service capacity

Enforce:

and:

Preserve deliberate discount ordering semantics.

13. Expose unallocated credit properly

Do NOT automatically offset/refund historical credit.

Admin must reconcile it deliberately.

14. Strong warning before approving large unallocated credit

If Admin is approving:

Payment: R4,250
Allocated: R300
Unallocated: R3,950

require explicit confirmation.

Example:

R3,950 of this payment is not allocated to an invoice.

Approving will record it as unallocated credit.

Require a reason where the remainder is non-trivial.

Never make accidental credit creation look identical to fully reconciled approval.
