5. Proper discount model

Add explicit invoice discount/adjustment support for:

Teacher/staff-child discount
Sibling discount
Custom approved discount

A discount is not a payment.

Example:

Tuition                R2,350
Teacher Discount      -R1,175
Net Amount Due         R1,175
Paid                   R1,175
Outstanding                R0
Status                  Paid

It must not appear as:

R2,350 due
R1,175 paid
R1,175 outstanding
Partial

if the approved discount makes R1,175 the actual amount due.
