---
name: Finance ledger integrity
description: Durable rules for reconciling invoices, payment allocations, carry-forward balances, and reversals.
---

Admin and Parent financial totals must come from the same authoritative ledger calculation. Billed amounts, allocated payments, unallocated credit, overpayments, arrears, and carry-forward balances must retain distinct meanings rather than being cosmetically reconciled.

Carry-forward invoices require explicit source-to-successor lineage. Original invoices remain as audit history but are excluded from active totals. Payment corrections use immutable compensating events with a database-enforced single-reversal invariant; they never delete history or adjust invoices by a broad month/year match.

Correction audits are part of the same database transaction as the reversal and replacement. They record the original, reversal, and every replacement transaction ID, including split credit rows, plus exact before/after values, the Admin, and the required reason. Parent notifications happen only after commit and remain non-fatal. A reversal fails closed if the invoice balance cannot support the full compensation.

Historical invoice charges and discounts must come from immutable invoice-line snapshots. Current service prices, enrollment flags, legacy discount flags, or transaction month/year metadata must never be used to reconstruct what an old invoice meant.

A line-less historical invoice may be allocated as legacy Tuition only when the invoice's own persisted description explicitly identifies Tuition or school fees. Missing Boarding or other service charges require an audited new charge; enrollment flags alone are never evidence of historical debt.

Parent choices, Admin allocation targets, and approval validation must use one persisted-evidence payable-obligation projection. A proof must be excluded from its own pending reservation check, but competing pending proofs still reserve their targets. Every explicit target requires a finite positive amount. An explicit empty target list means deliberate unallocated credit; a null plan alone means automatic oldest-unpaid allocation.

Every approved discount is capped by both remaining invoice capacity and remaining target-service capacity. Finance exports must use the same ledger projection and validated learner/period filters as the Admin view, with spreadsheet-safe text handling.

Classifying an existing ambiguous legacy invoice is metadata-only reconciliation of that same obligation: preserve its ID, header amounts, allocations, dates, and reference. Require zero existing line items, no active pending proof, no conflicting categorized allocations, and no carry-forward/source lineage. Classification and proof reservation must share the same ordered invoice locks.

Correct a mistaken legacy classification only by appending non-billable correction evidence. Keep the original charge immutable and make one effective-category resolver authoritative across payable, ledger, approval, allocation, Admin, Parent, and exports. Category-only historical proof selectors are ambiguous and must trigger Admin retargeting; they never reserve every invoice in that category.

**Why:** Aggregate carry-forward invoices and repeated payment edits can otherwise double-count debt, reverse unrelated allocations, or make Admin and Parent balances disagree. Separate payable logic can hide archived debt or approve stale targets. Conflating empty and automatic plans can acknowledge one disposition while executing another. Classifying an invoice concurrently with a proof, against conflicting historical allocations, or after carry-forward can duplicate or strand obligations. Editing original classification evidence destroys audit history; matching category-only proposals globally can make unrelated invoices appear pending. Current configuration can change after billing, and legacy totals do not prove a service category. Independent export formulas or filters can disclose unrelated learners or report stale balances.

**How to apply:** Allocate under transaction locks, target exact allocation and successor identities, exclude only the current proof from reservation checks, reject malformed explicit plans before database work, preserve the empty/null disposition distinction, share invoice locks across classification and proof reservation, fail closed on existing lines/pending proofs/category conflicts/carry-forward lineage, append zero-value included correction evidence without editing the original charge, resolve its effective category centrally, require stable invoice/line/fee/assignment identity for pending reservations, verify ledger projections before commit, write required correction audits before commit, notify parents after commit, snapshot approved charges and capped discounts when invoices are issued, use only persisted descriptions for narrow legacy Tuition compatibility, create audited missing-service charges prospectively, expose debt and unallocated credit separately, and test Parent/Admin/export equality across every payment channel.
